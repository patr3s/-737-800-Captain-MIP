# Complete PFD/ND front module assembly v1

## Front-to-rear stack

1. Dark fascia face segments, 1,0 mm.
2. Optional 0,4 mm fascia diffuser segments under future legends.
3. Light fascia base segments, 3,2 mm.
4. Two DU bezel v3 stacks installed in the fascia cutouts.
5. PFD and ND carrier frames behind the fascia.
6. BenQ front active plane aligned to the two carrier/bezel openings.
7. Fixed steel VESA 100 × 100 mm bracket attached to the structural MIP frame.

The steel bracket carries the monitor. Printed carrier/fascia parts establish XY alignment and light side contact only.

## Assembly datums

- Monitor-body top-left is `(0,0)`.
- PFD center: `(118,1; 134,0)` mm.
- ND center: `(327,9; 134,0)` mm.
- Carrier Y origin: 24,4 mm.
- Left carrier X origin: 0 mm.
- Right carrier X origin: 225,8 mm.
- Left bezel origin: `(16,5; 32,4)` mm.
- Right bezel origin: `(226,3; 32,4)` mm.

## Service sequence

1. Remove the front M3 bezel screws.
2. Remove bezel stacks and fascia segments as required.
3. Disconnect the independent COB/dimmer harness through the service connectors.
4. From the rear, disconnect VGA and power.
5. Support the monitor, loosen the steel VESA bracket and remove the monitor rearward.

Printed parts must never be used as the sole monitor support.
