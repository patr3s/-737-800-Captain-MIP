# PFD/ND alignment fit-check v1

## Purpose

This inexpensive coupon verifies the final center geometry before printing the complete 445 mm module:

- 6,6 mm physical carrier/bezel gap,
- 40,1 mm visible-opening gap,
- carrier opening edge,
- bezel stack edge,
- fascia center mask,
- M3 carrier joiner and fascia seam joiner.

No heat-set inserts are required. Use ordinary M3 through-screws, washers and nuts.

## Print quantities

Print one of every STL in this folder except this instruction file: 9 small parts total.

## Material and profile

- PETG, preferably the same Anycubic PETG/profile used for CAL-001.
- 0,4 mm nozzle, 0,20 mm layer.
- 4 walls, 5 top/bottom layers, 25 % gyroid.
- No supports, 100 % scale.
- Use the verified profile: 230 °C nozzle, 75 °C bed, flow 0,96, if the same grey Anycubic PETG is used.

## Assembly and measurement

1. Join `carrier_left` and `carrier_right` from behind with `carrier_joiner`; set the clear gap to exactly 6,6 mm before tightening.
2. Fit the two bezel coupons on the front carrier edges with M3 screws/nuts.
3. Insert the narrow fascia-center coupon between the bezel edges. It must enter without force and must not visibly rattle.
4. Measure the physical bezel-edge gap: target 6,6 mm.
5. Measure the two represented visible-opening edges: target 40,1 mm.
6. Assemble `fascia_seam_left` and `fascia_seam_right` with the rear seam joiner. The front faces must remain flush.
7. Photograph the assembly from the front, rear and side and record actual dimensions.

## Acceptance

- Carrier gap: 6,6 mm ±0,2 mm.
- Visible opening gap: 40,1 mm ±0,3 mm.
- Front fascia seam step: maximum 0,2 mm.
- M3 screws pass by hand through the 3,2 mm holes.
- No cracking or forced fit.

Do not print the complete fascia/carrier until this coupon is accepted.
